# Credits

## Tanpura recordings

**Pa tuning** — `public/tanpura/tanpura-*.m4a` are ~40–60 s segments of the user's own recordings:
"Tanpura (all scales)" by Amitava Sarkar, MUSIFINE (D#, E, F, G, G#, A, A#, B, C, C#, D) and
"Tanpura (F# Scale)" by Nawaraj Khatri. Used under the user's licence. The recording of the
user's own scale plays at its native pitch (only fine-tune cents applied).

Other tunings (Ma, Ni, Dha …) use the single-string recordings listed below.

## Tabla strokes

**Naad** by Oormi Creations — https://github.com/oormicreations/naad — used under the MIT License.

## Tanpura strings (any tuning)

`public/tanpura/strings/*.wav` — single-string tanpura recordings by **luckylittleraven** on Freesound,
**CC0** (public domain): [Tanpura note Low C sharp](https://freesound.org/s/416597/),
[Tanpura note C sharp](https://freesound.org/s/416605/), [Tanpura note G sharp](https://freesound.org/s/416598/),
[Tanpura note A sharp](https://freesound.org/s/416606/). Trimmed, loudness-matched, retuned at playback.

## Recorded theka

`public/theka/teentaal.wav` — cut per matra (ring extended with WSOLA) from
[Tabla Teentaal with Baya](https://freesound.org/s/525154/) by **tablage** on Freesound, licensed
**CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Retuned to the user's Sa at playback.
